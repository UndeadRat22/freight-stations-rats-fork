Version: 0.12.10
Date: 2026-11-06
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed "select new contents" on a blueprint containing rails shifting every rail one tile down and one tile right: the station-to-placer swap's half-tile offsets made the engine re-snap stored positions; blueprint entities are now stored verbatim (set_blueprint_entities with fix_positions=false).
    - Fixed a flipped or shifted paste leaving a stock balancer (or underground balancer) ghost stranded where no gap exists - for example inside what is or will be a station's footprint - where robots could never build it. Such ghosts are now refused after one retry, and the item is returned.
  Changes:
    - Stations can now stand beside the start of a curved rail segment: curved rails count as covering the tiles at the end where they meet the straight run (previously only straight rails counted, so any 6-tile span touching a curve was rejected).

Version: 0.12.1
Date: 2026-10-14
  Features:
    - Added fluid stations: long fluid tanks that sit beside a stopped train, level with its fluid wagons, placed and snapped like freight stations. Pumps and pipes connect anywhere along a station's long sides, and a sight glass shows its level.
    - Added fluid balancers: placed in the gap between two fluid stations, they join them into one shared fluid. Drag-building and blueprints fill the gaps automatically, and never join two different fluids.
    - Added underground fluid balancers, which join the fluid stations on both sides of the track into one shared fluid.
    - Added steel fluid stations, two tiles wide and holding a whole fluid wagon each, with their own steel fluid balancers and underground fluid balancers.
    - Hovering a fluid station outlines every fluid station it shares its fluid with.
    - New research: Fluid stations (after Freight stations and Fluid wagon) and Steel fluid stations.

Version: 0.10.1
Date: 2026-10-13
  Optimizations:
    - Linking, splitting and removing stations in large groups is two to four times faster.
    - Marking a whole group for deconstruction, or cancelling that, no longer freezes the game for seconds: the group is changed once instead of once per station.
    - The mod no longer runs every tick when it has nothing queued.
  Changes:
    - The deconstruction planner lists each tier's stations and steel stock balancers once, instead of once per group size and direction.
  Bugfixes:
    - Loading a save after changing mods no longer switches off recipes that other mods had unlocked by script.
    - Blueprints in the blueprint library updated with "Select new contents" can be rotated and flipped like any other blueprint with stations.
    - The placement guide and group outline are removed when their player leaves the game, and the guide comes back on rejoining while holding a station.
    - Stations another mod placed without telling the game now get linked when the save is loaded after a mod change, instead of all sharing one inventory. A station copied that way no longer takes the original group's contents.
  Compatibility:
    - Station placement collides exactly like the station itself, including collision layers other mods add, so it can no longer be refused on ground where the station would fit.

Version: 0.9.1
Date: 2026-10-12
  Optimizations:
    - The green placement guide is about twice as fast near busy train stations.
  Changes:
    - Mining a station from a group keeps its contents in the group when the remaining stations have room. You only get what no longer fits.
    - Splitting a group shares items out by whole stacks, in proportion to each part's size.
    - When robots take down part of a group, its contents move into the stations that stay, as far as they fit, instead of leaving with the dismantled part.
    - A station marked for deconstruction leaves its group at once and becomes a plain chest holding what the group can't keep. Robots empty it like a vanilla chest and then remove it, returning the station last, instead of leaving a carpet of single items on the ground. Cancelling turns it back into a station.
    - Loading a save re-checks every group against the stations and balancers actually placed.
  Bugfixes:
    - Mining a station that isn't linked to others no longer deletes its contents; they go to your inventory.
    - Splitting a group that holds many different items no longer spills part of them on the ground.
    - Fixed a crash when linking stations after another mod removed a balancer without telling the game.
    - Groups stay correct when another mod destroys a station without telling the game.
    - Steel stock balancers keep their quality when placed.
    - The group outline while hovering a station now updates when the group changes under the cursor.
